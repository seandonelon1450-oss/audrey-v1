Trust

Trust, plainly

  • Demo vs. real, labeled. Without an AI key, counsel answers come from built-in templates — clearly badged as demo. With a key set on the server, a real model answers instead, and the badge says so.
  • What you ask is not stored. Counsel questions exist only for the request, then they're gone. The server keeps no logs of what you asked and no database of your words. We do not train on your data.
  • Your Circle notes never leave this device. They live in this browser's local storage. We cannot see them, back them up, or recover them. Delete means delete.
  • No integrations. Audrey cannot see your calendar, email, or contacts, and cannot send anything on your behalf. The briefing screen is a design concept with fictional demo data, every item labeled Demo.
  • She advises; you decide. Audrey is counsel, not command. Nothing she says is financial, legal, or medical advice.
