FAQ

Asked plainly, answered plainly.

Does Arbor use AI?

Yes — honestly. Audrey drafts briefs and answers questions using AI: either built-in templates (labeled Demo) or a real model when one is configured on the server (the badge tells you which). Arbor for Law uses AI to assemble document drafts from your intake.

What we don't do: we don't pretend a person wrote it, we don't hide which answers are templated, and nothing sends, files, or pays without your tap. Every draft says drafted for you — review before sending. She advises; you decide.

Is the briefing real data?

No. The morning brief on this site is fictional demo data — every item is labeled Demo. Audrey has no calendar, email, or messaging access, and we say so on the screen. Real integrations will be claimed only when they're true.

Who can read my notes?

No one but you. Circle notes live only in this browser on this device. The server never sees them, they aren't backed up, and we can't recover them. Delete means delete.

What happens when I book a demo for Arbor for Law?

Your request (name, firm, email, and anything you add) is stored only so a real person from Arbor Technologies can follow up about the demo. You get a reference code; with it you can export or delete the request any time from Your data. No drip emails, no list-selling.

Is Arbor a law firm?

No. Arbor Technologies is a software company, not a law firm. It does not provide legal services, does not represent clients, and does not share in legal fees. The Arbor Group is a holding brand, not a law firm. Content on this site is general information, not legal advice.

Can I talk to a person?

Yes. There's a real human behind every Arbor product. Talk to a person any time.
