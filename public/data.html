Data charter

“You own your data”

  1. 1.Your data is yours. We hold it for you, to do work for you. Take it with you any time.
  2. 2.Never sold. Not to advertisers, data brokers, providers, or partners. No ad trackers.
  3. 3.Never used to train models. Not ours, not anyone's. The list of providers is public.
  4. 4.Export in one tap. Everything, in a readable format (PDF + JSON/CSV).
  5. 5.Delete in one tap. Gone from the live system right away, and from backups within 30 days.
  6. 6.Encrypted. In transit and at rest. Device-only rooms we can't read at all.
  7. 7.Collect the minimum. Each feature asks only for what it needs. Every connector is opt-in.
  8. 8.Your choice of home. Our encrypted cloud by default, or on your device only.
  9. 9.People see only what you share. Team access is limited, logged, and visible to you.
  10. 10.No quiet changes. New use of your data? We ask first, in plain words. Silence never counts as yes.
How this holds up today. No third-party pixels or trackers on this site. Counsel questions are never stored. Circle notes live only in your browser — we cannot see them. Demo requests are stored only to follow up on the demo, and you can export or delete them above with your reference code. Model-provider terms: demo counsel uses no model at all; live counsel uses the provider configured on the server, on no-training terms.
