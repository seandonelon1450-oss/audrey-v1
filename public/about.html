About

Your week, handled.

Audrey is a morning brief of finished work — drafts to approve, decisions to make, nothing to chase. She advises; you decide.

What's behind the name. A.U.D.R.E.Y. stands for Autonomous Unified Daily Routine Executive for You — the engine that assembles your brief, drafts your replies, and keeps your notes private. Day to day, she's just Audrey.
  • Finished work first. Audrey opens on a brief of what's ready — drafts with Approve, Edit, or Skip. Never a blank box waiting for you to do the work.
  • Demo is labeled. Anything fictional on this site carries a Demo flag. Real capabilities are claimed only when they're true.
  • Your data stays yours. The 10-point data charter applies to Audrey: export in one tap, delete in one tap, never sold, never used to train models. Read it.
  • A real person behind her. Questions? Talk to a person.
